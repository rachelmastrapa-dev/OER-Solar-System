Introduction

The last spacecraft that visited Uranus and Neptune was Voyager 2, which flew with technology from the 1960s and early 1970s. Since then, the high cost of outer planet missions has prevented the development of any new spacecraft. Astronomers argue that the scientific return for a mission to either planet would be tremendous considering the significant advances in technology since the days of Voyager 2. Until a new mission reaches the last two planets in the Solar System, we will do our best to fill this chapter with new information.

Learning Objectives
  • List the properties of Uranus and Neptune
  • Describe the composition and structure of Uranus' and Neptune's atmospheres
  • Discuss the features we observed on the surface of Triton when Voyager 2 flew by
