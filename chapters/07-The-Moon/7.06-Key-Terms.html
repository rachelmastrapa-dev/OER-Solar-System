The Moon Key Terms

  • giant-impact hypothesis: a proposed model for the formation of the Moon based on the impact of an object the size of Mars that ejects material into space that eventually forms the Moon
  • lunar highlands: the area of the Moon that is higher in elevation, lighter in color, more heavily cratered, and older than the maria
  • mare: the dark areas on the near surface of the Moon that were once thought to be seas, but are now known to be basalt lava flows
  • volatile: a type of elements or molecules that can easily vaporize, volatility depends strongly on pressure and temperature conditions, can be used as a noun or an adjective
