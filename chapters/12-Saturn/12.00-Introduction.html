Introduction

Saturn may be smaller than Jupiter, but its large, beautiful rings make it worth a closer look. While it has similar physical properties to Jupiter, it has some slight variations that create different structures, such as the unique hexagon. Saturn's moon Titan is the only moon in the Solar System with an atmosphere, and Enceladus may harbor liquid water. Let's review the properties of Saturn.

Learning Objectives
  • Describe the design and history of the Cassini spacecraft
  • List the properties of Saturn
  • Describe the composition and structure of Saturn's atmosphere
  • Explain how the thick atmosphere of Titan makes bodies of liquid on its surface possible
  • Describe the two theories of planetary ring formation
  • Compare the major rings of Saturn
  • Describe how ring structure is affected by the presence of moons
