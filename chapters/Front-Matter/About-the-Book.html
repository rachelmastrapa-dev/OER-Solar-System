OER for Stellar Astronomy

wanted to create a textbook focused on stellar astronomy

Approach

This textbooks is written for a college level introductory course about the stellar and galactic astronomy, with the assumption that the course has no math or science prerequisites.

Science and Society

Each chapter in this textbook ends with a page discussing the interaction of astronomy and human society. Each page discusses a topic related to the chapter content including past and present events with links to more information. These sections are designed to encourage students to think about their own connection to the field of astronomy.

This Calculus textbook is part of the De Anza College Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) Pathways initiative, supporting both students and instructors with free, adaptable Open Educational Resources (OER). This De Anza College ZTC Pathways Calculus textbook offers a clear and accessible introduction to limits, differentiation, and their applications, including tangent lines, implicit functions, and parametric curves. It is the first in a four-book series designed to build a strong conceptual foundation in calculus while connecting theory to real-world applications in fields such as physics, economics, and engineering. This series builds upon the OpenStax Calculus foundation and acknowledges the contributions of Lake Tahoe Community College’s derivative version, incorporating videos and interactive elements. De Anza’s version enhances accessibility, adds multimedia resources, and emphasizes critical thinking and problem-solving for both classroom and independent study. With its blend of open access, practical applications, and rich multimedia support, this textbook reflects De Anza College’s commitment to student equity, innovation, and leadership in OER. In addition to the core text, we provide ancillary materials to enhance teaching and learning.
