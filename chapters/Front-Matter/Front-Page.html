Textbook Summary

This textbook is designed for an introductory, general education astronomy class about Solar System astronomy. It was developed as part of the De Anza College Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) Pathways initiative to provide free, accessible course materials for students. The material has been organized to cover the topics of the course while supporting the needs of students from a broad range of backgrounds. Topics include the origins and development of astronomy, stellar and planetary formation and evolution, and comparative planetology, with each chapter including a page connecting science and society.
