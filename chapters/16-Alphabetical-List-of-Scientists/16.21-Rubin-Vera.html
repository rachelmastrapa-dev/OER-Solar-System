Vera Rubin

Vera Florence Cooper Rubin was an American astronomer who studied galaxy rotation rates. She uncovered the discrepancy between the predicted and observed angular motion of galaxies by studying galactic rotation curves. She concluded that the galaxies were rotating at rates that should make them fly apart. This was the first evidence for dark matter. Measurements by other astronomers using 21-centimeter hydrogen line radio telescopes confirmed the result.
