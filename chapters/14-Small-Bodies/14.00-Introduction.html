Introduction

This chapter is a collection of all of the other objects in the Solar System. The IAU definition of small Solar System body is any object that orbits the Sun and is smaller than a dwarf planet. Although the name of the chapter is Small Bodies, we also include the dwarf planets.

Learning Objectives
  • Describe the composition and classification of the various types of asteroids
  • Explain the threat that near-Earth objects represent for Earth
  • Discuss possible defensive strategies to protect our planet
  • Describe the origins of meteor showers
  • Explain the origin of meteorites and the difference between a meteor and a meteorite
  • Describe the general physical appearance of comets
  • Explain the range of cometary orbits
  • Describe the traits of the centaur objects
  • Describe trans-Neptunian and Kuiper-belt objects
  • Compare the orbital characteristics of Pluto with those of the planets
