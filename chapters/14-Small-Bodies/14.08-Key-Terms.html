Small Bodies Key Terms

  • asteroid: a stony or metallic object orbiting the Sun that is smaller than a planet and formed during the formation of the Solar System
  • asteroid belt: the region of the orbital plane of the Solar System located between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter which is occupied by numerous minor planets (asteroids) and the dwarf planet Ceres
  • C-type asteroid: C is for carbonaceous, the most common type of asteroid, probably consist of clay and silicate rocks, and are dark in appearance, are among the most ancient objects in the Solar System
  • centaur: an object that orbits the Sun between the orbit of Jupiter and Neptune, they often cross the orbit of either Jupiter or Neptune
  • dwarf planet: objects that orbit the Sun, and are nearly round, but have not been able to clear their orbit of debris
  • iron meteorite: a meteorite composed primarily of iron and nickel
  • M-type asteroid: M is for metallic, usually nickel-iron, their compositional differences are related to how far from the Sun they formed, some experienced high temperatures after they formed and partly melted, with iron sinking to the center and forcing basaltic lava to the surface
  • meteorite fall: when a meteor is observed and the associated meteorite is located soon after
  • meteorite find: when a meteorite is found somewhere on Earth
  • primitive: material that has not experienced great heat or pressure and therefore remains representative of the original condensed materials from the solar nebula
  • S-type asteroid: made of silicate materials and nickel-iron
  • stony meteorite: a meteorite composed mostly of stony material, either primitive or differentiated
  • stony-iron meteorite: a type of differentiated meteorite that is a blend of nickel-iron and silicate materials
