Venus Key Terms

  • corona: structures unique to Venus that may be caused by plumes of rising material from the mantle and are characterized by their circular shape and tectonic deformation of their surrounding areas
  • greenhouse effect: when gases in an atmosphere absorb infrared photons released by a surface heated by visible sunlight, retaining heat that could have escaped to space
  • tectonic: features such as cracks, faults, and scarps that are created by extensional, compressional, or lateral forces in the crust
