About This Textbook

This textbooks is written for a college level introductory course about the Solar System, with the assumption that the course has no math or science prerequisites.

Science and Society

Each chapter in this textbook ends with a page discussing the interaction of astronomy and human society. Each page discusses a topic related to the chapter content including past and present events with links to more information. These sections are designed to encourage students to think about their own connection to the field of astronomy.

Accessibility

Since astronomy is a visual science, a large part of this project has been ensuring that the textbook meets ADA and WCAG accessibility requirements. Figures have brief alternative text embedded within the image. For more complex figures, there is a link in the figure caption to an accessible description at the end of the chapter. These descriptions are a detailed visual description combined with an overview of the learning outcomes of the figure. Other accessibility features were designed for improving screen reader functionality, including nested headers on each page, tables formatted with headers, limited use of bold or italics, and removal of long text strings such as URLs, with the exception of APA formatted references. Language has been simplified for effective translation and localization.

Note that some pages have a Further Exploration note box with external links. These links are for students to find interesting information, but are not screened for accessibility or licensing. They are not required components of the textbook.
