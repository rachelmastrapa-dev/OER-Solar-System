Introduction

Named after the messenger of the Roman gods, Mercury is the smallest of the planets and is the closest planet to the Sun. Because it is so close to the Sun, its entire surface should be very hot. As we will learn, its slow rotation results in a huge range in temperature between its day and night side. Although very similar to the Moon, there are some important differences between the two objects, which we will review in this chapter.

Learning Objectives
  • List the properties of Mercury's orbit around the Sun
  • Explain the relationship between Mercury's orbit and rotation
  • Describe Mercury's structure and composition
  • Describe the topography and features of Mercury's surface
