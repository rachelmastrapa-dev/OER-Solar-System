Introduction

This is numbered chapter zero because it is not meant to be part of the main part of the textbook. Think of this as a resource for finding information about math and science that you might not be familiar with. It is also here in case you would like a little more information about a specific topic. There are also some tips and tricks for getting the most out of this textbook. Consider this chapter a reference that you can check when you are not familiar with a concept in the main text.
